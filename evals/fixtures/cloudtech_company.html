About CloudTech Solutions

CloudTech Solutions is a cloud infrastructure company founded in 2020. The company specializes in helping businesses migrate to and manage cloud infrastructure across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Our headquarters is located in Seattle, Washington, with satellite offices in Denver and Boston.

The company currently serves 150 enterprise clients and employs 85 cloud engineers, architects, and support staff. In 2025, CloudTech Solutions achieved $12 million in annual recurring revenue, representing 40% year-over-year growth.

Our mission is to simplify cloud adoption and help organizations build resilient, scalable infrastructure. We focus on three pillars: migration services, managed cloud operations, and cost optimization consulting.

Services and Pricing

CloudTech Solutions offers three main service packages:

1. Cloud Migration Service

Complete migration planning and execution for businesses moving from on-premises to cloud infrastructure. Our team handles assessment, architecture design, data migration, and testing. The migration service starts at $25,000 for small deployments and scales based on complexity.

2. Managed Cloud Operations

24/7 monitoring and management of cloud infrastructure with guaranteed 99.95% uptime SLA. Includes automated scaling, security patching, backup management, and incident response. Monthly retainer starts at $5,000 for basic infrastructure.

3. Cost Optimization Program

In-depth analysis of cloud spending with recommendations to reduce costs without sacrificing performance. Typical clients see 30-40% cost reduction in the first year. Engagement fee is $10,000 with performance-based bonuses for savings achieved.

All services include dedicated account management and quarterly business reviews. We maintain partnerships with all major cloud providers and pass volume discounts to our clients.
